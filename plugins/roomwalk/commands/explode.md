description: Build a scroll-driven product hero — one object on black that comes apart as you scroll
---

Load the `exploded` skill and follow it.

Check that the Higgsfield tools (`generate_image`, `generate_video`) are in this
session first. If they are not, run `/roomwalk:connect` and stop.

Then ask for two things in one line: what the product is, and a photograph of it.
If the product is real and purchasable, the base image must be the client's own
photograph — generate the explosion from it, never the product itself.
